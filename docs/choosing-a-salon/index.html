The 90-second check

Marble counters photograph well. Hygiene habits protect you. Five things visible from the doorway tell you which one a salon invested in.

1 · Single-use tools, opened in front of you

Files, buffers and cuticle sticks cannot be sterilised and should be fresh for every client. The best salons make a small ceremony of tearing the pack open where you can see it.

2 · A steriliser you can see working

A UV cabinet or autoclave behind the desk, lights on, metal tools inside. It is the single strongest signal in the room, because it is money spent where clients rarely look.

3 · Prices in writing, before you sit

A posted menu · including removal · means the salon runs on process, and process is what hygiene is made of. Cross-check against our fair-price tables; honest menus land inside them.

4 · Products with names

Ask what gel system they use. Korean and Japanese brands dominate the quality end here, and proud salons answer instantly. Anonymous decanted pots are your cue to leave.

5 · Air you can breathe

Gel and acrylic work produce fumes. Ventilation that handles them tells you the owner cares about everyone in the room, including the technicians who live in it twelve hours a day.
