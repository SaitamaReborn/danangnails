Nail salon with head spa in Da Nang

Nails in the morning and a head spa somewhere else in the afternoon is two Grabs and two waits. These places do both under one roof.

Where can I get my nails done and a head spa in the same place in Da Nang?

Reborn Nails & Retreat is our first choice. Its full menu of eight Vietnamese herbal head spa rituals (25 to 105 minutes, 120K to 850K) sits beside gel, BIAB, GelX and spa pedicures, and since two technicians can work at the same time, both get done in one sitting (4.9★, 306 Google reviews, 2 October 2026). The list has 11 places, ours first, then those whose Google name shows both nails and a hair wash.

A hair wash and a head spa are not the same booking

Plenty of Da Nang nail salons add a shampoo: a wash, a short scalp massage, a blow-dry, often from about 100K. A head spa is a longer ritual built around the scalp, with a herbal soak, steam, masks and neck and shoulder work, sometimes facial care, from about 45 minutes to well over an hour.

If you only want clean hair after the beach, any place on this list will do. If the head spa is the treat of the trip, look for a menu with several rituals and the minutes written next to each.

Fitting both into one visit

Ask for two technicians. At Reborn Nails & Retreat a manicure and a head, neck or foot massage can run in the same sitting, according to the salon's menu. Message on WhatsApp to book that, especially for a weekend afternoon.

We kept every venue in our Google Places snapshot (2 October 2026) whose listing name mentions nails together with a shampoo, hair wash or head spa, in English, Vietnamese or Korean. If you book only one

For nails and a proper head spa in one afternoon, book Reborn Nails & Retreat at 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, 880 m from My Khe Beach. A good pairing: the Reborn Signature head spa (80 minutes, 500K) and a gel colour (200K).

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a manicure and a head spa at the same time in Da Nang?

Yes, where two technicians are free. At Reborn Nails & Retreat a manicure and a head, neck or foot massage can run in the same sitting.

How much is a head spa at a nail salon in Da Nang?

At Reborn Nails & Retreat the head spa menu runs from 120K for a 25-minute herbal wash to 850K for a 105-minute ritual; the 80-minute Reborn Signature is 500K. A plain hair wash added to a nail visit elsewhere often starts around 100K.

What is gội đầu dưỡng sinh?

The Vietnamese name for the restorative herbal hair wash: a reclined double shampoo with herbs such as grapefruit peel or lemongrass, a scalp massage and, in longer rituals, steam, masks and neck and shoulder work.
