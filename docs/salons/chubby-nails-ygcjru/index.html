★★★★★★★★★★

My go-to nail salon whenever I’m in Danang (pretty much half the year). I originally chose this location because it’s super close to my hotel but ended up always coming back even though there are so many other options. The owner here is really friendly and her girls are super sweet. I’ve been going here for about a year already. She’s upgraded her pedicure chairs to couches which I love! There are not many salons…

Rosanna Tran · 6 months ago · via Google

★★★★★★★★★★

The staff were friendly and the pedicure was fine, but the manicure felt a little rushed even though I was the only customer. It was also uncomfortable having to stretch my right arm across while getting my nails done. After I got home, I noticed quite a few bubbles, some uneven texture, and what looks like a small hair or fibre trapped inside one of my nails... The price is very affordable, so overall it was ok…

Icy K · a month ago · via Google

★★★★★★★★★★

I did my acrylic nail extensions here 3 weeks ago and the retention is so good! I did a lot of packing and carrying activities this past week, and even accidentally knocked into a box, but the extensions and my nails remained so sturdy. There’s no lifting at all. Binh was the artist who did my nails and she was quick, thorough and her painting is neat. Simple English communication is fine however it would be good…
