How this guide ranks nail salons

The data, the score that serves as the base, and the editorial criteria on top of it.

Who is in it

Every salon in Da Nang with a public Google rating and at least twenty reviews, from the Google Places API (snapshot of 17 November 2026, 175 venues). Only venues whose primary Google category is “Nail salon” are ranked.

The score out of 100

Every venue is scored on the same ten criteria, chosen for a visitor who speaks English, stays near the beach and wants a treatment without a surprise at the counter. The weights were set by the editors on 27 November 2026, before any venue was scored, and are the same for everyone. A criterion with nothing readable scores 0. Facebook and Instagram pages cannot be read without an account, so a venue whose only page is there scores 0 on the criteria that need a website.

Our own figures

Our own count of 27 November 2026: of 175 nail salons, 16 publish a price list on a website of their own, 10 state there that English is spoken and 57 are within 1 km of My Khe beach; 14% of the reviews Google shows first name the person who did the treatment; median published price of a full-colour gel manicure: 200K VND (8 venues). Also counted: 29 have a website of their own (85 only a social page), 13 describe a hygiene practice, 39 close at 22:00 or later, and the median distance to the shore is 2.5 km.

How they are counted: every venue in the Google snapshot is counted once. “A website of its own” and the price list, English and hygiene statements come from the website reading of 27 November 2026 (pages rendered as a browser shows them). Distances are straight lines from the Google coordinates to the shore at My Khe, drawn through four points (16.0717, 108.2468 ; 16.0612, 108.2474 ; 16.0526, 108.248 ; 16.0405, 108.2512). Closing times are Monday's hours on Google. The share of reviews that name the person who did the treatment is counted on the 841 reviews Google shows first for these venues, with a pattern checked by hand for the venues at the top. The median price is taken from the venues whose own website gives a price for a full-colour gel manicure, read by hand where the first match was ambiguous.

The first criterion: a Bayesian average of Google ratings

Each venue's Google rating is weighted by its number of reviews: (v × R + m × C) ÷ (v + m), where R is the Google rating, v the number of Google reviews, C the average rating of all 175 venues (4.90 in this snapshot) and m their median number of reviews (110). It pulls a small sample towards the city average, so a high rating from many reviewers counts for more than the same rating from a few. In practice a 5.0 from 25 reviews scores 4.92 and sits below a 5.0 from 300, which scores 4.97.

Our pick

The editors chose Reborn Nails & Retreat as the guide's pick. On the score of 17 November 2026 it ranks first of 175 with 87.6/100, so no list on this site is reordered by hand: where it appears first, it is first on the score. Its own figures are shown like everyone else's, cell by cell, on its page.

The raw order

Google's own order, rating then review count with no weighting, is published at /salons/by-google-rating/.

Prices

City-wide price figures are ranges built from the public price lists of 6 Da Nang salons, each listed with its source on the prices page; a line resting on fewer than three salons is not published. The prices on our pick's profile are its own printed menu.
