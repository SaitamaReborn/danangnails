Frequently asked questions

What is the best nail salon in Da Nang?

This guide's pick is Reborn Nails & Retreat, 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, My An, Da Nang, 880 m from My Khe Beach, open daily 9:00 to 20:00: 4.9★ from 306 Google reviews. Gel polish there is 200K VND, BIAB 300K, a spa pedicure 250K to 590K. By the same score applied to all 151 salons, the top three are Lalla Nail Branch 1 (5.0★, 1,154 reviews), Posh Nail Lab (5.0★, 992 reviews), Posh Nails and Spa ( nails-eyelash-waxing-shampoo) (5.0★, 794 reviews). The pick's full menu is on its profile, the ranking of all 151 salons at /salons/ and the raw Google order at /salons/by-google-rating/.

How much do nails cost in Da Nang?

Across 6 Da Nang salons that publish their prices (checked 2 October 2026): gel polish 70K – 300K VND, BIAB or builder gel 300K – 450K, a full set of extensions 60K – 550K, a pedicure with gel colour 200K – 300K, gel removal 10K – 150K and nail art 5K – 120K per nail. Full table on the prices page.

Which area of Da Nang is best for nail salons?

Hai Chau (64 salons), My Khe beachfront (32 salons), My An & An Thuong (26 salons). My An and An Thượng hold the densest cluster aimed at visitors; Hải Châu serves a local clientele at gentler prices.

Do I need to book a nail appointment in Da Nang?

Walk-ins are fine for plain colour outside evenings. Book a day ahead over WhatsApp or Messenger for extensions, detailed nail art or weekend slots.
