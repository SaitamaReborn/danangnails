Da Nang's nail scene, street by street

The city's salons cluster in a handful of areas, each with its own price gravity and clientele. Pick your area first and the salon almost picks itself.

My An & An Thượng · the visitor's cluster

The blocks between Ngũ Hành Sơn's beach entrances and the An Thượng grid hold the densest concentration of quality salons in the city. English is spoken, menus are posted, Korean and Japanese gel systems are the norm, and spa-style pedicure lounges sit two doors from ten-seat neighbourhood shops. Expect city-average prices with a mild premium on the busiest lanes.

This is also where our featured salon operates · the corner of the market where certified technicians and single-use kits are table stakes.

The beach road · convenience at a premium

Võ Nguyên Giáp and the seafront blocks price for their postcode: the same gel set runs 10–30% above the city average. Worth it when you want wet hair, salt skin and a manicure inside the same hour · just know what the premium buys, which is location, not better product.

Hải Châu · where locals go

Across the river in the administrative heart, salons serve a Vietnamese clientele at gentler prices. Menus may be Vietnamese-only and hand-painted art leans toward local trends. If you enjoy pointing at a design and letting skilled hands work, some of the city's best value hides here.

Sơn Trà · quiet and residential

North along the peninsula road the pace drops, shops thin out, and you will often have a salon's full attention. Fewer English menus, honest prices, and a slower rhythm that suits long rituals more than quick colour.

Wherever you land, run the 90-second hygiene check before you sit down. Neighbourhood predicts price · it does not guarantee practice.
