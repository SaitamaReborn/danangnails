Không sơn: 18 reviews in 841 mention a man, and one of them leaves with colour on

Four of the six Da Nang menus behind the price table clean hands or feet with no colour on them, for 70K to 120K. The two that print a men's section start at 260K, and at Liti the same four steps cost 30K more under the men's heading than under the other one.

In short
  • What most men want is the cheapest service on the board, and there is no colour on it. That line appears on four of the six Da Nang menus behind the price table. On its own site, Reborn Nails & Retreat answers the manicure question with a 70K classic manicure; LENA Nails & Beauty sells a 100K manicure billed at 20 to 30 minutes; SuNam Nail & Spa, 12 Phan Bôi charges 100K for nail and pedi care over 15 to 25 minutes and marks the line men and women; Amor Spa has a basic manicure at 120K. All four menus were read on 19 November 2026.
  • The word men's buys a bundle, not a service. Liti Nail & Hair prints five men's lines and none of the five includes polish: 310K for a manicure or pedicure with heel care, 260K to 410K with a 30 or 60 minute foot massage, 380K for hands, feet and heels together, 340K to 490K with a massage instead, and 590K for the lot. LENA prints two, 380K for 60 minutes and 480K for 75. Set Liti's two lists side by side and its men's mani, pedi and heel care is 380K where the same four steps are 350K on the other one.
  • Eighteen of the 841 public reviews behind this guide mention a man, and they are spread across 17 of the 175 salons. In eleven of the eighteen, his own hands or feet go under somebody's tools. The others put him in a massage chair, under a hair wash, in a barber's chair, or sitting with the shop dog. Just one review in 841 has a man walking out with colour on: at Thao Nails, 253 Nguyễn Chí Thanh, a customer who got acrylics says her boyfriend had a new gel design done.
  • This piece has one opinion: order by the steps, never by the heading. Ask for the basic manicure and say không sơn, add heel work only if a week in sandals has earned it, and do not buy the haircut. The only one-star review in the whole male set is a cut and a perm at Liti on Lê Quang Đạo, where the customer says her boyfriend's cut came back with an unblended line. A nail shop with a hair chair is not a barber.

Eighteen reviews, and one of them has colour on it

This guide rests on a snapshot dated 17 November 2026: 175 Da Nang nail salons, 59,527 Google ratings and 841 review texts. Of those texts, eighteen mention a man. They fall across 17 different salons, roughly one shop in ten, and in eleven of the eighteen someone is working on his own nails or feet. Nothing in the set suggests anyone is turned away. Men just are not written down here, so the question gets asked at the door rather than answered in advance.

What they leave with is modest, and it repeats. At Azit Nail, 15 Nguyễn Thái Học, 4.9 across 1,350 ratings, a reviewer notes that her husband had a manicure too and that his hands no longer look busted. The praise at NAIL SWEET, 43 An Thượng 3 is for cleaning up his toes. At Pink Nail Studio, 167 Trần Phú, which by the reviewer's account is up on the sixth floor above a shop, the husband had the nail scrub and no polish. The clearest account of the order itself comes from Yumi Nails Studio, 147 Nguyễn Chí Thanh: her partner had never had a professional cuticle cut before, the shop speaks little English, and she ends by recommending a clean manicure for men or women.

Men wrote two of the reviews, about their own hands. At Nails & Spa By Yam, 18 Chu Văn An, a first-timer recommends the place to men who want their nails cleaned up or who have come along with a girlfriend. A party of three took a cab to TUYEN NAILS & SPA in Sơn Trà at 8.30pm with no booking, were served anyway, men included, and put a photo of the bill in the review. The latest of the eighteen, dated 7 November 2026, is at BoNa Nail, 336 Phan Châu Trinh: a customer says the owner has looked after her partner's nails, and his arm and leg hair removal, for eight months across two business names.

Colour is the exception. It happens once. At Thao Nails on Nguyễn Chí Thanh, 4.9 across 1,942 ratings, an October 2026 review has her in acrylics and him in a new gel design, both done quickly and, by her account, with care. Another Thao Nails review puts a husband, a son and a mother in the chairs at the same time: the son's first time, the husband on the package with the foot massage. For a price that can really be checked against a bill, Hoàng Oanh Beauty & Nails, 90/10 Trần Phú did her nails and her partner's nail care in two hours for around 650K, her pedicure included, booked over Instagram and handled through a translation app.

Where it happens is not where the tourists are. Of the 17 salons, eleven are in Hải Châu, the inland district with 69 of the 175 listings and most of the local trade. Two sit in My An and An Thượng, the beach streets where visitor money goes. On this evidence, a man who wants his nails tidied walks into a neighbourhood shop that is not putting on a show for anybody.

The line with no colour on it is 70K to 120K

Read the six Da Nang menus the price table rests on and four of them sell a clean-up with nothing applied afterwards. Amor Spa is the most literal about it: basic manicure 120K, nail trimming, shaping, cuticle care and nail cleaning, and a basic pedicure at the same 120K with foot cleaning and moisturising. LENA's manicure is 100K and billed at 20 to 30 minutes, with trim and shape, cuticle care, nail cleaning and a light hand massage. SuNam's version runs 15 to 25 minutes at 100K, says men and women on the line, and finishes with a protective layer of paint. Reborn's own site puts a classic manicure at 70K. Those are four salons' published figures, read on 19 November 2026, not a city average.

Nail Bơ, 122A Bắc Sơn goes further and sells the steps one by one: cắt da, the cuticle work, costs 20K alone, and phá gel, removing old gel, is 10K. One customer there itemised her visit in Vietnamese: 170K for cat eye on hands and feet, the old gel taken off and the nails cleaned. This is the floor of the market. It is in a shop nobody flies in for.

Nothing here is among the six lines on the price table, since the table measures what gets applied: gel colour at 70K to 300K across all six menus, a gel pedicure at 200K to 300K across four, removal at 10K to 150K. Under all of that sits the clean-up, and nobody tabulates it. It is short, too. Fifteen to thirty minutes, against the sixty to ninety a gel set takes, and that matters if the rest of the party is still picking a colour.

What helps is knowing which part of the twenty minutes you are paying for. It is not the file. Any barber can shorten a nail. The thing a Da Nang nail technician does that nobody does at home is work the skin at the base with nippers, and that is the step the Yumi Nails customer's partner had never once had done. Everything else is finishing.

What the heading adds, and what it is for

A men's section appears on two of the six menus. Liti's runs to five lines, and not one of them includes polish: manicure or pedicure with heel care from 310K, manicure or pedicure with a 30 or 60 minute foot massage at 260K to 410K, manicure plus pedicure plus heel care from 380K, the same with a massage instead at 340K to 490K, and hands, feet, heels and a massage together from 590K. Its step lists cover nail trimming, cuticle cleaning, heel callus removal and moisturising care.

Hold that 380K line against the one two headings above it. Liti's ladies' list also sells manicure plus pedicure plus heel care, described in the same four steps, from 350K. Its pedicure with heel care is 300K on that list and 310K on the men's. So the men's version of an identical step list runs 10K to 30K higher, which is somewhere between 40 cents and a dollar twenty, and not worth an argument. It is worth knowing, because it tells you the heading is a packaging decision rather than a different service. Order the steps and the question disappears.

LENA's two men's combos go the other way. Combo 1 is a manicure plus a Deluxe Pedicure at 380K for 60 minutes; buy those two lines separately off the same price list, which its FAQ dates to 1 November 2026, and it is 100K plus 300K, so the bundle saves 20K. Combo 2 is the manicure with the VIP 4-in-1 foot spa at 480K for 75 minutes against 500K apart. Twenty thousand đồng either way. LENA also adds 4% for card payment, so bring the cash and the saving survives.

What you are actually paying the difference for is heels, and that is a fair trade for a lot of people. Liti's standalone heel care and callus removal is 250K and comes with a soak, a scrub, machine callus removal, a mask, a massage and moisturiser. A week of sandals on My Khe does real damage, and the soak is a separate line here anyway. If your heels are fine, the 310K line is 210K of foot spa you did not need.

The only dedicated men's address in the city is not a nail salon. Brothers Men's Salon, at 178 Trần Phú in the Wink Hotel building, publishes 330K for a classic dry manicure or pedicure: nail trimming, shaping, cuticle care, a warm sea salt soak and a hand and arm or foot and calf massage, all in a shiatsu chair. Opening days are Tuesday to Sunday, 9am to 7pm, and it is closed Mondays, the one fact in this piece you can get wrong and lose an afternoon to. Because it is a barber shop with a nail service, it is not among the 175 listings here. Exactly one of those 175 has barber on the sign: Heaven Spa&Nail&barber, 227 Nguyễn Văn Thoại, 4.8 across 1,021 ratings. Fifty-five of the 175 name some second trade on the sign, and 21 of those name hair.

Không sơn, said before the colour wheel comes out

The whole appointment needs three phrases. Không sơn means no polish. Cắt da is the cuticle work, which is what you came for, priced at 20K alone at Nail Bơ. Làm sạch móng means cleaning the nails; a customer used it to describe her own visit in a public review, so it will be understood as written. Say không sơn early. Colour is the default at a Da Nang nail salon, the colour wheel shows up while your hand is still in the bowl, and a polite nod at the wrong moment is how men leave with a clear top coat they never asked for.

Expect the nippers. A first cuticle cut is a surprise even when it is done well, there is pressure at the base of the nail, and the right question is whether it hurts rather than whether it should. The skin half of the appointment has its own rules, and the one word that stops any tool in this city is đau.

Watch the tools while you are there, because a 100K appointment gets the same shortcut a 400K one does. A three-star review at Azit Nail, the same shop whose husband left happy, says no new file was opened for her, the nail cutter went in without being sanitised, and she walked out with the left hand cut shorter than the right. The cuticle nippers did get sprayed. The door checks take ninety seconds and they are the same checks whoever is in the chair.

Two last orders worth naming. Liti sells buff and shine as a 40K add-on per set, which is the difference between tidy and visibly shiny, and most men want the first. And heels get machined here: a regular at CHUBBY NAILS, 73 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, in Da Nang about half the year, singles out the electric filer they run over dry patches. If you want the nails short, say the length as a number, because short means four different things in this room.

The other chair, and the one thing not to buy in it

When he is only there to wait, the shop would rather sell him something, and those prices are published. A foot massage at Liti is 200K for 30 minutes and 350K for 60, and a hair wash with head and shoulder massage and a blow-dry costs 100K. SuNam has a 60 minute foot massage at 260K. Reborn's basic hair wash is 120K for 25 minutes. At HQ Nails Spa, 79/1 Nguyễn Hoàng, a couple booked the deep gội đầu dưỡng sinh package together at 120K each, and the reviewer wrote up the whole sequence.

In the reviews, the same thing happens by accident. At Nail villa, 79 Đỗ Bá, the husband had an hour-long massage during her manicure and pedicure, and the shop takes bookings over KakaoTalk. The boyfriend at Local Beach Spa&Nail, 151 Nguyễn Phan Vinh had a full body massage in a private room inside the nail salon while his own broken gel nail was being matched. At CHUBBY NAILS he had a foot spa and a shoulder massage, and reported the massage as very strong. Over at CIAO NAIL STUDIO 2, 05 Nguyễn Thông, the husband spent the ninety minutes under inspection by Moon, the shop's black poodle, and wrote the review himself.

Refuse the haircut. Just one review in this whole set has one star, and it is about hair: at Liti on Lê Quang Đạo, in September 2026, a customer says her boyfriend's cut came back with an unblended line, the perm was acceptable, and her own 13-dollar manicure and gel pedicure package had bubbles in the gel, nails not cleaned underneath and cuticles that, she says, were never pushed. When she was told through Google Translate that they had been, the shop offered a redo it would not guarantee. Twenty-one of the 175 salons here have hair on the sign, and most of them are a shampoo basin rather than a barber. A bad set of nails can be taken off for 10K to 150K. With a bad haircut, you wait for it to grow.

Frequently asked

Can men get their nails done in Da Nang?

Yes, and nothing in the public record says otherwise. Eighteen of the 841 reviews behind this guide mention a man, spread over 17 different salons, including Azit Nail at 15 Nguyễn Thái Học, NAIL SWEET at 43 An Thượng 3, Yumi Nails Studio at 147 Nguyễn Chí Thanh and Nails & Spa By Yam at 18 Chu Văn An, where the reviewer is a man writing about his own first manicure. Colour is the genuinely rare part: one review in 841 has a man leaving with gel on, at Thao Nails on Nguyễn Chí Thanh.

How much is a men's manicure in Da Nang?

For the plain clean-up with no polish, 70K to 120K, which is what four of the six salons behind this guide's price table publish: 70K at Reborn Nails & Retreat, 100K at LENA Nails & Beauty for 20 to 30 minutes, 100K at SuNam Nail & Spa on Phan Bôi for 15 to 25 minutes and 120K at Amor Spa. Lines printed under a men's heading cost more, since they bundle heel work or a massage: Liti Nail & Hair charges 260K to 590K across five men's lines, LENA 380K for 60 minutes, and Brothers Men's Salon at 178 Trần Phú 330K for a dry manicure or pedicure. Menus were read on 19 November 2026.

What do I ask for if I do not want polish?

Say không sơn, no polish, and say it before the colour wheel arrives. Ask for the basic manicure, which is what Amor Spa calls it on its own menu, or point at the nail and pedi care line marked men and women if you are at SuNam. Cắt da is the cuticle work with nippers and it is the part worth paying for, 20K on its own at Nail Bơ. Add buff and shine only if you want the nails to catch the light; Liti charges 40K per set for it.

What can my partner do while I get my nails done in Da Nang?

Several things in the same room, all of them on published menus. A 30 minute foot massage is 200K at Liti Nail & Hair and 60 minutes is 350K, SuNam sells 60 minutes at 260K, a hair wash with head and shoulder massage is 100K at Liti and 120K for 25 minutes at Reborn. Reviews put partners in a private-room body massage at Local Beach Spa&Nail on Nguyễn Phan Vinh and an hour-long massage at Nail villa on Đỗ Bá. The one thing to skip is the haircut: the only one-star review in this set is a cut and perm at Liti on Lê Quang Đạo.
