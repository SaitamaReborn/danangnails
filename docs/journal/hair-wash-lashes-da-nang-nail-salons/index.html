Gội đầu, nối mi, waxing: the second trade named on 28 of 151 Da Nang nail shops

Fourteen Da Nang nail shops advertise a hair wash in their own listing name and ten advertise lashes. In the 715 reviews behind this guide the shampoo comes up 27 times, the lashes twelve. One salon publishes both price lists, and the distance between 70K and 700K is the whole piece.

In short

Twenty-eight names with a second job in them

Read the shop names in this city as a menu, because that is what they are. Posh Nails and Spa, 6 An Thượng 9, 794 reviews, carries the string nails-eyelash-waxing-shampoo inside its own listing title. R Nail & Spa 1 at 1 An Thượng 4, 485 reviews, gets five trades in: nail, eyelash, eyebrow, shampoo, waxing. Tiệm Nail Con Ong, out at 01 Đinh Văn Chấp in Hòa Xuân, uses the Vietnamese: nail, gội đầu, uốn mi, which is a hair wash and a lash perm.

Across all 151 nail salons in this guide, 28 do it. Fourteen advertise the hair wash and ten the lashes; seven put hair in the name, six a massage, two waxing, two brows. A few are blunter than the rest. She Nails at 113 Trần Duy Chiến in Sơn Trà writes the hair wash twice, once in English and once in Korean, 릴렉스 샴푸, for the customer who needs it put that way. At Lyn Nail Pmu, 72 Hoàng Dư Khương in Cẩm Lệ, gội đầu comes before the nails and cosmetic tattooing after them. Heaven Spa&Nail&barber at 227 Nguyễn Văn Thoại, with 1,017 reviews, squeezes a barber in.

This is no branding accident. A name on a Google listing is free advertising space, and these shops treat it as a board outside the door, since the business is one room, two or three technicians and whatever those technicians can do. On the An Thượng streets where salons sit a couple of doors apart, the second trade is how one of them wins your afternoon over the one next to it.

A name, though, is not a price list. Posh's title promises four services. Its published menu prices manicures and pedicures (Posh Manicure 120K, Deluxe 250K, gel colour 120K on top, gel removal 50K, pedicures 200K, 350K and 550K), but the tabs labelled Hair Wash and Artificial Nails hold no items at all. The sign is a claim; the number comes later, usually in a message.

Gội đầu: 70,000 đồng, and the service nobody flies in for

Search the 715 reviews for anyone who had their hair washed and 27 come back, which makes the shampoo the most documented non-nail service in the dataset by a factor of two. Lashes and brows together manage twelve.

The two numbers I can source for it are low. A reviewer at TiNa Nails, 71 Nguyễn Công Sáu in An Hải, writes out shampoo 70,000vnd and neck and shoulder massage and then says everything was perfect. Liti Nail & Hair, whose menu is one of the six behind the price table, publishes hair wash and head massage from 100,000 đồng with styling excluded, a facial with the wash included from 350K, a women's cut with a wash from 400K and a men's cut with a wash from 200K. Nobody publishes a duration for the wash, which is a shame, because the length is the point of it.

The reviews do not describe a rinse. At Ruyi Nail Art, 29 Phan Bội Châu, a customer who has had her hair washed there many times calls it the most delicate wash she has had and notes two details that show what kind of room it is: there is a curtain if you want privacy, and nobody talks loudly. One visitor at Lee Nails & Spa, 74 Tô Hiến Thành (441 reviews) went in for a gel manicure with a design and came out having added a hair wash and a scalp massage; the review adds that the shop is cheaper than others nearby and walkable from a lot of hotels. At Mia, 05 Mạc Thiên Tích, it was nails and a hair wash in a single sitting, with an owner who, according to the reviewer, speaks English very well.

Someone at Thiên Ban Nailroom, 9 Bàu Sen 1 in Thanh Khê found the best use of it. Waiting to go to the airport, she had her nails done and a hair wash treatment, and all she wants you to know is that the shop is very close to it. In one line, that is the whole argument for the gội đầu: it is the cheapest way to turn dead time into something.

One dissent, and it is worth having. At Heaven Spa&Nail&barber, a reviewer found the hair washing and scalp massage quite rough, said so plainly, guessed it might be a local technique rather than a mistake, and still thanked the staff for their kindness. That is the realistic downside: 70K and a firmer pair of hands than you expected. Compare it with the ways a 550K nail set can go wrong and you see why I would risk the wash. Some of this work turns up unbilled anyway, but the wash is a line you have to ask for.

Nối mi: the dearest line in the shop, priced by one salon

Ten of the 151 salons put lashes in the name. Minh Tâm Lashes & Nail on Dũng Sĩ Thanh Khê leads with them. Others include Darling Nail & Eyelash, 24 Tôn Thất Tùng, Red Rose on Trần Bạch Đằng, Dung Mie Nails & Nối Mi on Phan Thanh and KEL ART NAIL at 50 An Thượng 5, which carries eyelash, head spa and massage after the name. One of the ten publishes what a set costs.

Here is Liti's list: classic synthetic 400K to 500K, classic in a natural look 550K to 650K, a mixed set from 500K, katun from 550K, volume from 500K, hybrid from 600K, foxy, anime, eyeliner-effect and wet-look sets from 600K, douyin from 650K, mega volume from 700K. Lower lashes are 70K. A refill runs 200K to 360K, with removal from 60K, a lash lift from 300K and a lift with tint from 400K. Put the bottom of that next to the city's published gel colour range of 70K to 300K, and the cheapest lash set in the building still costs more than the dearest gel manicure.

Durations come from elsewhere, because the nail shops do not print them. Bee Honey Spa, a separate spa at 19 An Thượng 26 in Bắc Mỹ Phú, prices its lashes in dollars, which tells you exactly who it expects: a classic set at 60 minutes, listed at 30 dollars with the page currently showing 27, and a design set at 90 minutes for 34. Thao Eyelash at 125 Trưng Nữ Vương in Hải Châu does lashes and nothing else, publishes a 45-to-75-minute window for extensions, a 300,000 đồng lash lift and brow lamination at 400K to 600K, and still does not publish the price of the extensions themselves.

Across 715 reviews, exactly one person volunteers a lash figure, and it comes bundled: at CIAO NAIL STUDIO 2, 05 Nguyễn Thông, a visitor paid SGD 80 and change for lash extensions and custom nails together, got a free miso coffee thrown in, and was delighted. From the customer side, that is the entire public record of what lashes cost at a Da Nang nail salon.

So here is the flag. It is the most expensive item on the menu and the least documented, it goes on an eyelid, and the shop doing it may have one lash artist and a Facebook page. Before you turn up, get the number and the style name into the message thread where you book. A gel colour you dislike grows out. But a lash set you dislike sits on your face for three weeks, and removing it is another 60K and another appointment.

What can be done at once, and what only looks like it can

Lash work puts you flat on your back for somewhere between 45 and 90 minutes with your eyes shut. Your feet are idle for all of it. So are your hands. That is the whole case for stacking, and the shops that are set up for it do exactly that.

CASA NAIL SPA, 42 Mai Thúc Lân (326 reviews) took three people with no appointment, was told they were short of time, and got manicures, pedicures and lashes done for all three in under two hours. A first-timer at CIAO NAIL STUDIO, 22 Mai Thúc Lân, 652 reviews, had nails and lashes in one visit with two technicians on her hands at once; she says the English was good enough that she stopped worrying, and notes the shop is cash-only (one of the staff lent her a raincoat to walk to the ATM). By a reviewer's account, a family was served all at once at Ciao's second branch, nails, pedicures and lashes together.

The compression comes from staff and not from the service list. Two technicians on one customer is the standard lever here, so a shop with four pairs of hands can do your feet and your eyes in parallel. A shop with one cannot. At Minh Tâm Lashes & Nail the owner did the nails and the lashes herself, and the review praises her light hands at both, a compliment about skill rather than time. One customer had been hunting specifically for a place doing both mani-pedi and leg waxing and found it at MIRA B Nails, K35 on Tô Hiến Thành, where the review spells out the catch. The owner does everything herself, so book ahead: they do not take walk-ins.

Choosing is the one thing that really does not stack. With your eyes closed and lash tape on your lids you cannot look at a reference photo, and the photo on your phone is doing more work than your English is. Before you lie down, settle the nail design, the shape and the colour. In my experience of how these rooms run, the order that works is this: design agreed, lashes and feet together, hands after, hair wash last, since the wash needs your head back at a basin and by then nothing on your nails minds water.

Waxing, brows, a barber, and the men's column nobody mentions

The menu's tail is longer than the lashes. Liti lists brow shaping at 150K, brow tinting at 150K, lamination from 400K and lamination with tint and shaping at 600K. Waxing runs: upper lip 100K, eyebrow 150K, full face 300K, armpit 200K, half legs 350K to 700K, full legs 600K to 1.2 million. A women's haircut with a wash starts from 400K, a man's from 200K.

The men's line is no rounding error. Liti prints a men's combo column: a manicure or pedicure with heel care from 310K, both with heel care from 380K, both with a 30 or 60 minute massage at 340K to 490K, and everything together from 590K. On its own, heel care and callus removal is 250K; a 30-minute foot massage is 200K, an hour 350K.

The reviews back this up. At Azit Nail, 15 Nguyễn Thái Học (1,349 reviews), a woman reports that her husband had a manicure too and that his hands no longer look busted. A couple turned up at CHUBBY NAILS, 73 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, half an hour before closing, and both were taken. At Lalla Nail, 8 An Thượng 29, 1,154 reviews, a party of three split three ways: acrylics with a Crayon Shin Chan design and a pedicure for her, floral builder gel for her daughter, and the heel care pedicure for her husband. R Nail & Spa 1 on An Thượng 4 has the cleanest bill in the set, itemised by a reviewer: his plain manicure 80K, her gel removal plus an ombré French manicure plus the heel scrub, exfoliation and foot mask package 750K, 830K for the pair. The shop's published menu, incidentally, is a file on Google Drive.

The second trade has a quieter use as well: the person who came along and is not buying nails at all. At Heaven Spa&Nail&barber, a man waiting an hour and a half for his girlfriend's nails took the back massage in the meantime and slept through it. Two friends went into Reborn Nails & Retreat, 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, for a full-body massage and a hair wash instead of nails, and call it the best two hours of the trip. Often the nail shop is the most comfortable waiting room on the street.

Stacking can also buy a discount, as one review shows. At Cong Nail Da Nang, 80 Trần Phú, 395 reviews, a customer who had used the massage services got the heel scrub, normally around 150K, for 20K, plus twenty per cent off; her services and her husband's came to under 300K in total. She also saw a nail drill go from her husband's feet straight to another customer's manicure with nothing done to it in between, and wrote that down as well. A bundle discounts the price. What it does not discount is the ninety seconds you spend reading the room.

Read the sign, then ask for the number

Three sentences in a booking message cover all of it. Name every service you want in one list instead of adding them in the chair. Ask whether two technicians are free at that hour, since that decides if your two hours become one. Then request the total with the lash style named, because that line has the widest spread and the least public pricing.

After that, take the cheap one. The hair wash costs 70K at a twenty-review shop on Nguyễn Công Sáu and from 100K at a chain with six branches and a published menu. It is the single most mentioned extra in 715 reviews, and the worst anyone has said about it in public is that it was firmer than expected. No other 70K purchase in the Da Nang beauty trade has that record.

Ten shops in this guide put lashes on the sign. One puts them on a price list. Until that ratio changes, read the name as an advertisement and treat the message thread as the menu.

Frequently asked

Can I get my nails and eyelashes done at the same place in Da Nang?

Yes, and ten of the 151 nail salons in this guide advertise lashes in their own listing name, including Minh Tâm Lashes and Nail on Dũng Sĩ Thanh Khê, Darling Nail and Eyelash at 24 Tôn Thất Tùng, Red Rose on Trần Bạch Đằng and KEL ART NAIL at 50 An Thượng 5. Whether it saves you time depends on staff rather than on the menu. CASA NAIL SPA at 42 Mai Thúc Lân took three walk-in customers who said they were short of time and finished manicures, pedicures and lashes for all three in under two hours, and a first-timer at CIAO NAIL STUDIO, 22 Mai Thúc Lân, had nails and lashes done with two technicians working on her hands at once. At a one-chair shop the same stack simply runs back to back. Lash work means 45 to 90 minutes flat on your back with your eyes shut, so settle the nail design and the colour before you lie down, and expect to pay 400K upwards for the lashes on top of the nails.

How much do eyelash extensions cost in Da Nang?

Just one salon in this guide publishes a list. Liti Nail and Hair, whose nail menu is one of the six behind this guide's price table, prices classic synthetic lashes at 400K to 500K, classic in a natural look at 550K to 650K, volume from 500K, hybrid from 600K, mega volume from 700K, a refill at 200K to 360K, removal from 60K and a lash lift from 300K. On durations, Bee Honey Spa at 19 An Thượng 26 lists 60 minutes for a classic set at 30 dollars (its page currently shows 27) and 90 minutes for a design set at 34. Thao Eyelash at 125 Trưng Nữ Vương does only lashes, gives a 45 to 75 minute window and a 300,000 đồng lash lift, and publishes no extension price. Across 715 public reviews, one customer gives a figure at all: SGD 80 and change at CIAO NAIL STUDIO 2 on Nguyễn Thông, for lash extensions and custom nails together. Ask for the total by message, with the style named.

Do Da Nang nail salons wash your hair?

Many do, and fourteen put it in the shop name as gội đầu, shampoo or hair wash. Among the 715 reviews behind this guide it is the most mentioned non-nail service, with 27 mentions against twelve for lashes and brows. At TiNa Nails, 71 Nguyễn Công Sáu, a reviewer puts a shampoo with a neck and shoulder massage at 70,000 đồng; Liti Nail and Hair lists hair wash with head massage from 100,000 đồng, without styling, and a facial with the wash included from 350K. Reviewers describe a reclining basin, a long scalp massage and often neck and shoulder work, and Ruyi Nail Art on Phan Bội Châu has a curtain if you want privacy. One dissenting review, at Heaven Spa and Nail and barber on Nguyễn Văn Thoại, found the wash and scalp massage rougher than expected. At 70K to 100K on top of a manicure, it is the best-value twenty minutes in the trade here, yet almost no visitor asks for it.

Do Da Nang nail salons do men's manicures and pedicures?

Routinely, and one chain prints a column for men. Liti Nail and Hair lists a manicure or pedicure with heel care from 310K, both with heel care from 380K, both with a 30 or 60 minute massage at 340K to 490K and the full combination from 590K, as well as a men's haircut with a hair wash from 200K. The reviews support it: a husband's manicure at Azit Nail on Nguyễn Thái Học, a couple both taken half an hour before closing at CHUBBY NAILS on Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, a heel care pedicure for the husband while his wife had acrylics at Lalla Nail on An Thượng 29, and an itemised 830K bill at R Nail and Spa 1 on An Thượng 4 that covers his 80K plain manicure and her 750K gel and heel package. If he is only there to wait, most of these shops sell a massage or a hair wash at the same counter; one reviewer's partner did just that at Heaven Spa and Nail and barber while she had her nails done.
