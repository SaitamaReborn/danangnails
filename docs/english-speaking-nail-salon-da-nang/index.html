What English-speaking means in a Da Nang salon

In the tourist quarters, most technicians manage the basics in English: colour, shape, length, price. Anything that needs explaining is harder, for example why a BIAB overlay suits short nails, what removal will cost next time, or whether a chrome design will survive a week of sea water. For that conversation you need someone at the desk who can hold it.

Screenshots and a translation app cover the rest. Bring a photo of the shape and colour you want; every salon on this list works from references.

How we built the list

For each of the 151 salons in this guide, we read the Google reviews returned by the Places API (up to five per salon, snapshot of 2 October 2026) and kept the ones where a reviewer mentions English. This shows that a reviewer found it worth mentioning. It is not a language test, and a salon absent from this list may well speak English too. The editors place our pick first, for the reasons above.

Three questions to send on WhatsApp first

Send the photo, ask for the price including removal, and ask how long it takes. A salon that answers all three clearly in English is one you can talk to at the chair.
