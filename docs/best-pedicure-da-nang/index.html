How is this list made?

Same dataset and same method as the rest of the guide: 156 Da Nang salons with a public Google rating and twenty or more reviews, ordered by a score weighing the rating against the size of the sample, applied to every salon alike.

For pedicure specifically, judge a menu on minutes. A 250K express ritual buys forty minutes; a 590K signature buys seventy-five plus hot stones. Both are honest prices for what they contain — the adjectives on the board are not.

The score, applied to all 156 nail salons without exception: (v × R + 120 × C) ÷ (v + 120), where R is the Google rating, v the number of Google reviews and C the average rating across all 156 venues (4.90 in this snapshot). This page shows the top 10. Our pick is shown in its own box above the list and is scored exactly like the rest: on this snapshot it is 82nd of 156. The complete ranking is at /salons/ and the untouched Google order at /salons/by-google-rating/.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get the best pedicure in Da Nang?

This guide's pick for pedicure in Da Nang is Reborn Nails & Retreat, 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, My An, 880 m from My Khe Beach, open daily 9:00 to 20:00: 4.9★ from 306 Google reviews. How much is a pedicure in Da Nang?

Express rituals start around 250,000 VND for about forty minutes. A full 55–65 minute ritual runs 380–450K, and a 75-minute signature with hot stones about 590K. Gel polish for toes adds roughly 180K.

What does a spa pedicure in Da Nang include?

A warm herbal foot soak, cuticle care and nail shaping, heel buffing and intensive heel treatment, exfoliation, a hydrating mask, foot and calf massage, a warm towel wrap and nourishing oils. Longer tiers add hot stones and more massage time.

Is a spa pedicure worth it over a basic one?

If you have been walking a beach city in sandals, yes. The heel therapy and the massage are exactly the parts a basic trim skips, and they are the reason the ritual takes forty minutes rather than fifteen.

Do pedicures in Da Nang include a foot massage?

In any proper spa pedicure ritual, yes — foot and calf massage is part of the sequence from about 250K upward, not a separate charge. Check what the ritual contains before paying for a massage on top.
