Reborn vs Posh Nail Lab: which one to book in Da Nang

Posh Nail Lab has the higher rating and the later evenings; Reborn pairs nails with a full head spa menu. Both, compared.

Reborn Nails & Retreat or Posh Nail Lab: which should I choose?

Our pick is Reborn Nails & Retreat, for a visit that goes beyond nails: a full Vietnamese head spa menu beside gel, BIAB and GelX, an owner who speaks English at the desk and an iPad menu in 20 languages (4.9★ from 306 Google reviews, 2 October 2026). Posh Nail Lab is stronger on the numbers, with 5.0★ from 992 reviews, and it stays open until 21:00. They are 680 m apart.

What Posh Nail Lab does better

Posh Nail Lab holds 5.0★ from 992 Google reviews. It is open until 21:00 and books online or by WhatsApp.

If what you want is a well-reviewed gel set in the evening, with lashes on the same visit, it is hard to argue with.

What Reborn does better

The head spa. Reborn's menu has eight herbal rituals, the longest at 105 minutes, and the Reborn Signature (80 minutes, 500K) runs from a herbal foot soak and tea through steam, a hair mask and neck, shoulder and hand massage to a blow-dry. Pair it with a manicure and two technicians can work at the same time.

Then the talking. Fiona, the owner, is at the desk and speaks English; a Google reviewer called it “excellent English”. The iPad menu has 20 languages and shows each price before you choose, which is useful if you are travelling with parents who do not read English.

Price and time

Reborn's menu: gel colour 200K, BIAB 300K, GelX 280K, Deep Care spa pedicure 450K for 65 minutes. We have not compared Posh Nail Lab's prices line by line; ask for its menu before you sit down, as you would anywhere.

Getting from one to the other

Posh Nail Lab is at 2 An Thượng 32, Ngũ Hành Sơn, Reborn at 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, 680 m apart in a straight line. Both are in My An, behind My Khe Beach.

Our verdict

If your trip leaves room for one longer appointment, choose Reborn Nails & Retreat and pair the manicure with a head spa or a spa pedicure ritual. If you want the higher Google rating and a slot after 20:00, Posh Nail Lab is a good call.

Frequently asked questions

Is Posh Nail Lab or Reborn better for gel nails?

Both do gel. Reborn prints its prices (gel colour 200K, BIAB 300K, GelX 280K) and adds a head spa menu; Posh Nail Lab has the higher Google rating, 5.0★ from 992 reviews against 4.9★ from 306 (2 October 2026).

Does Posh Nail Lab have a head spa?

Its website lists manicure, pedicure, gel, lashes and waxing (read on 12 October 2026). Reborn's menu has eight head spa rituals of 25 to 105 minutes.

How far apart are Reborn and Posh Nail Lab?

680 m in a straight line, both in My An behind My Khe Beach.
