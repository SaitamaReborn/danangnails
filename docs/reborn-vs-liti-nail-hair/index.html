Reborn vs Liti Nail & Hair: which one to book in Da Nang

Both do nails and both wash hair. The difference is how far each one takes it.

Reborn Nails & Retreat or Liti Nail & Hair: which is better for nails and a hair wash?

When the head spa matters, we pick Reborn Nails & Retreat: eight rituals of up to 105 minutes, an owner who speaks English at the desk and an iPad menu in 20 languages (4.9★, 306 Google reviews, 2 October 2026). Liti Nail & Hair has seven addresses in Da Nang. Its An Thượng 39 branch holds 4.9★ from 1,307 reviews, and its hair wash with head massage starts at 100K, below Reborn's shortest wash. The distance between them is 490 m.

Where Liti is stronger

Scale and convenience. Liti Nail & Hair has seven addresses across Da Nang, so there is usually one near your hotel, and its An Thượng 39 branch alone holds 4.9★ from 1,307 Google reviews. Its prices are on its website, its hair wash with head massage starts at 100K, and it stays open until 20:30.

For a quick wash and blow-dry after the beach, or a gel set close to where you are staying, it does the job well.

Where Reborn is stronger

Depth of the head spa. At Liti, a hair wash means a wash with a massage. Reborn's head spa menu has eight rituals, and the 80-minute Reborn Signature (500K) moves through a herbal foot soak and tea, facial cleansing, a quartz-stone massage, herbal steam and a mask, scalp exfoliation, a double herbal wash, a hair mask, neck, shoulder and hand massage and a blow-dry, then fruit and tea.

It is also one salon, with the owner, Fiona, at the desk, speaking English, and a menu on the iPad in 20 languages.

Getting from one to the other

Liti's branch is at 08 An Thượng 39, Ngũ Hành Sơn, Reborn at 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, 490 m apart. Both sit in My An, a short walk inland from My Khe Beach.

Our verdict

Choose Reborn Nails & Retreat when the head spa is the reason for the visit, or when you want nails and a long herbal ritual in one sitting. Liti Nail & Hair is the pick for a quick hair wash, or for a salon close to wherever you are staying, since it has seven.

Frequently asked questions

Does Liti Nail & Hair do head spa?

It offers a Hair Wash & Head Massage from 100K (its website, 12 October 2026). Reborn's head spa menu has eight rituals, from 120K for 25 minutes to 850K for 105 minutes.

Which is closer to My Khe Beach?

Liti's An Thượng 39 branch sits deeper in the streets behind the beach; Reborn is 880 m inland from My Khe at 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế. They are 490 m apart.

Which has more reviews?

Liti Nail & Hair at An Thượng 39 has 1,307 Google reviews at 4.9★; Reborn has 306 at 4.9★ (2 October 2026).
