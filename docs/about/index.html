About this guide

Who writes it, where the numbers come from, and who pays for what.

What this is

The Da Nang Nail Guide is an independent editorial site about getting your nails done in Da Nang, Vietnam · prices, neighbourhoods, treatments and the practical judgement calls the brochures skip.

Where prices come from

Every figure is compiled from menus posted publicly by salons across the city and refreshed as we re-walk the districts. Prices are typical ranges, not quotes; individual salons set their own.

Editorial rules

  • We never publish invented reviews, ratings or salon listings.
  • Star ratings shown for any salon are its public Google rating, nothing else.
  • Price tables reflect posted menus, not negotiated or promotional rates.

Partner disclosure

Our featured salon, Reborn Nails & Retreat, is a commercial partner of this guide. Its rating (4.9 from 150+ Google reviews) is its real public Google rating, and the practices we praise it for · single-use kits, posted prices, certified technicians · are the same criteria we apply to every salon in these pages. Partnership buys placement; it does not buy the criteria.
