Reborn vs Lalla Nail: which one to book in Da Nang

Two salons a few minutes apart in My An, with different strengths. Here is what each one does better, with the figures.

Reborn Nails & Retreat or Lalla Nail: which is better?

Our pick is Reborn Nails & Retreat: it pairs gel, BIAB and GelX with a full Vietnamese head spa menu, the owner speaks English at the desk, and its iPad menu runs in 20 languages, at 4.9★ from 306 Google reviews (2 October 2026). Lalla Nail Branch 1 is the one to choose if the rating decides it for you: 5.0★ from 1,154 reviews, and it stays open until 21:00. The two are 680 m apart.

Where Lalla Nail is stronger

Numbers first. Lalla Nail Branch 1 holds 5.0★ from 1,154 Google reviews, one of the largest samples of any nail salon in the city, and it closes at 21:00, an hour after Reborn. If your plan is a gel colour after dinner, or you simply trust the bigger sample, it is a sound choice.

It also sits on An Thượng 29, inside the grid of small streets where many visitors to My An stay, so for a lot of readers it is the nearer door.

Where Reborn is stronger

Reborn is built for the longer visit. Its menu puts gel, BIAB and GelX next to four spa pedicure rituals and eight Vietnamese herbal head spa rituals, from a 25-minute wash at 120K to a 105-minute ritual at 850K. Two technicians can work at once, so nails and a head spa share one sitting instead of two appointments.

The other difference is the conversation. The owner, Fiona, runs the floor, and a Google reviewer wrote that she “speaks excellent English”. The menu on the salon's iPad comes in 20 languages, Korean, Chinese, Japanese and Russian included, with each price on screen before anything starts.

Getting from one to the other

They are 680 m apart in a straight line. Reborn is at 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, 880 m inland from My Khe Beach; Lalla is at 8 An Thượng 29, Ngũ Hành Sơn, closer to the sand. A Grab between the two takes a few minutes.

Our verdict

On a first visit to Da Nang, and especially if you want your nails done and your hair washed in the same afternoon, book Reborn Nails & Retreat. Lalla Nail Branch 1 suits you better if all you want is a gel colour late in the evening and you go by the rating.

Frequently asked questions

Is Reborn Nails & Retreat better than Lalla Nail?

For a visitor who wants nails plus a head spa or a pedicure ritual, with an owner who explains everything in English, we recommend Reborn (4.9★, 306 Google reviews, 2 October 2026). On rating and review volume alone, Lalla Nail Branch 1 is ahead with 5.0★ from 1,154 reviews.

How far is Reborn Nails & Retreat from Lalla Nail?

680 m in a straight line. Reborn is at 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế and Lalla Nail Branch 1 at 8 An Thượng 29, Ngũ Hành Sơn, both behind My Khe Beach.

Can I get a head spa at Lalla Nail?

Its website lists hair washing as an add-on to nail services. For a full herbal head spa ritual, Reborn's menu runs from a 25-minute wash at 120K to a 105-minute ritual at 850K.

Which one is open later?

Lalla Nail Branch 1 until 21:00 and Reborn until 20:00, both every day, according to their Google listings.
