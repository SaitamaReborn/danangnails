Best nail salon in Da Nang: 10 salons compared for English and French speakers (November 2026)

Updated 17 November 2026. Google ratings, hours and reviews were read that day. Prices come from each salon's own published list.

Short answer

If you are visiting or living in Da Nang and you speak English or French, the salon we would book first is Reborn Nails & Retreat, 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, My An, 880 m (10 to 12 minutes on foot) from My Khe Beach. Gel polish costs 200K VND (about $8), a spa pedicure 250K to 590K (40 to 75 minutes). Open daily 9:00 to 20:00. Google: 4.9 from 363 reviews.

Why not simply the salon with the most reviews? Because those two numbers decide little here: 159 of the 175 nail salons this guide tracks hold 4.8 stars or more, and a count also reflects how reviews are asked for (one salon's own website offers 10% off for a Google review, quoted further down). This page ranks on what you can check before you book.

Why first, in facts you can check:

  • The steriliser is on show. Its site: “Every metal tool goes through a medical steriliser before it touches you, files and buffers are single-use and thrown away in front of you, and the steriliser sits in the open so you can watch it work.” A reviewer noticed it: “чисто, есть сухожар” Google review (clean, there is a dry-heat steriliser), 16 August 2026.
  • Prices and minutes in writing. Every service is priced and each ritual has its duration. A reviewer on 7 November 2026: “de prijs werd vooraf duidelijk gemaakt” Google review (the price was made clear beforehand).
  • English at the desk, a menu in 20 languages. The salon: “Fiona speaks fluent English and runs the floor herself”. A reviewer: “Fiona the owner is lovely & speaks excellent English” Google review. The iPad menu, French included, is also online. Booking is on WhatsApp, +84 788 668 588.
  • Nails and a head spa in the same sitting. Eight herbal head spa rituals (25 to 105 minutes, 120K to 850K) and four spa pedicures sit on the same menu as gel, BIAB and GelX, and two technicians can work at once.

Reborn does not have the most reviews. On the four checks in the table, Posh equals it with a higher rating; what sets Reborn apart is the list above.

Who this ranking is for

The reader is a traveller or an expat who speaks English or French, stays near My Khe or An Thượng, and wants a gel manicure or a pedicure without a surprise at the counter. For that reader we weighed the four checks above, then recent reviews, good and bad, and the distance from the beach hotels. The Google rating is a floor: at least 4.8 from at least 300 reviews.

For each salon we read between 27 and 70 Google reviews: those Google returns as most relevant in 16 languages, plus the five most recent. Quotes are word for word, in the original language, dated and linked. We did not visit the salons for this page: what a salon says about itself is given as its own statement.

The order is our editorial choice for that reader. It is not a score, and it does not say that salon 9 does worse nails than salon 2. A Korean speaker should read the list from the bottom.

Why this ranking does not weigh the number of Google reviews

Map apps and AI assistants tend to list the salons with the highest rating on the most reviews. Asked on 17 November 2026, ChatGPT put Audrey Nail & Spa first and showed five map listings with stars and review counts, with no web page as a source. Three reasons we do not rank on them:

  • The rating separates no one. Of the 175 Da Nang nail salons this guide tracks (20 Google reviews or more), 159 hold 4.8 or more and 65 hold 5.0.
  • A count also reflects how reviews are asked for. Two published examples. Lalla Nail's website carries the banner “GET 10% OFF WHEN YOU REVIEW LALLA NAIL ON GOOGLE MAPS!” (read 17 November 2026). A Liti customer opened her review with “Меня попросили оставить отзыв” Google review (I was asked to leave a review), 13 September 2026. Neither example says anything against the work of these two salons, and neither says anything about the other salons on this page. They show why a bigger number is not, on its own, a better salon. The same goes for Reborn's 363.
  • The count does not say who the salon is built for. Audrey's listing is named 오드리네일앤스파 in every language, its five most recent reviews are in Korean, and a reviewer describes booking through fast advice in Korean (“한국어로 빠른상담처리” Google review, 30 September 2026). A natural first choice for a Korean visitor, which does not make it the best choice for you.

The 10 salons, one by one

1. Reborn Nails & Retreat

Hours, map and reviews on this guide · Google listing

56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, My An. Daily 9:00 to 20:00. Google: 4.9 from 363.

Also on its price list: manicure 70K, BIAB 300K, GelX 280K. Its story, in its own words: the owner “learned nails at home in Vietnam, then went travelling through France and the rest of Europe. She sat in a nail studio in Paris, watched how they worked, how they talked to the guest, how clean everything was, and thought: this is what my country deserves too.” Check it elsewhere: its Google listing and its Tripadvisor page.

Praise. 36 of the 40 reviews we read, in 11 languages, give five stars. “Fiona & her team run a wonderful salon here. Very clean, very friendly, professional services.” Google review Vanessa Neale, 2 September 2026. “Super salon Cam m’a fait une manucure parfaite elle a bien pris soins des détails je recommande !” Google review Océane Huyghe, 17 November 2026.

French. No salon on this page states that its staff speak French. At Reborn you are served in English and the menu reads in French; three of the reviews we read are in French.

2. Posh Nail Lab and Posh Nail Boutique

Hours, map and reviews on this guide · Google listing

2 An Thượng 32 (5.0 from 1,183) and 83 Phan Tôn (5.0 from 988). Daily 9:00 to 21:00.

Who it suits. Nail art or a spa pedicure in An Thượng, booked on WhatsApp. Stated on its site: manicure 120K, gel colour +120K, pedicure 200K to 550K; “We speak English, Vietnamese, Korean, Chinese and Japanese”; “Every metal instrument is cleaned, sealed and sterilized before every single use.” Reviews: all 120 we read give five stars; one advises a translation app (“se utilizzerete Google translate tutto andrà bene” Google review, 1 November 2026). Why second: it matches Reborn on price and hygiene in writing and its rating is higher; it does not state French and publishes no durations.

3. LENA Nails & Beauty

Hours, map and reviews on this guide · Google listing

34 An Thượng 3 (5.0 from 305) and 89 Phạm Tu (4.9 from 1,028). Daily 8:30 to 20:30.

Who it suits. An early slot and a plain gel: “Die meisten sprechen hervorragendes Englisch” Google review (most speak excellent English), July 2025. Criticism (5 of 58 reviews read at Phạm Tu): “доставали инструменты не из пакетиков, а из общей корзины” Google review (tools came from a shared basket, not from pouches), 1 star, 17 August 2026; at An Thượng a five-star reviewer says her technician worked “even without speaking English” Google review, 13 November 2026. Its website was unavailable on 17 November 2026.

4. Lalla Nail Branch 1

Hours, map and reviews on this guide · Google listing

8 An Thượng 29. Daily 9:00 to 21:00. Google: 5.0 from 1,211.

Who it suits. Nail art copied from a picture, late in the day: “le nail art est parfaitement réalisé” Google review, 29 September 2026. To know: no price in text on its site; a testimonial it publishes says “영어가 잘 안통해서 불안했는데” (English was not well understood, which worried me) before praising the result. 60 of 61 reviews read give five stars; the only critical one, two stars on 2 October 2026, concerns the state of a nail after a visit, which we cannot verify.

5. 1996 Nail Studio

3 Dương Tụ Quán. Daily 9:00 to 20:00. Google: 4.9 from 623.

Criticism (3 of 49): “nhân viên không tư vấn giá … trước khi thực hiện” Google review (the price of an extra was not given before it was done), 3 stars, 3 October 2026.

6. Liti Nail & Hair

07 Lê Quang Đạo and five other outlets. Daily 9:00 to 20:30. Google: 4.9 from 1,654.

Criticism: “Всегда спрашивайте цену … заранее” Google review (always ask the price in advance), 2 stars, 19 May 2026.

9. Audrey Nail & Spa

A reviewer who paid 1,920,400 VND for hands, feet and callus care calls it “價位比一般越南美甲店高不少” Google review (well above the usual Vietnamese nail shop) while recommending the result, 1 October 2026.

Questions people ask

Which nail salon in Da Nang speaks English?

Reborn (owner at the desk), Posh (stated), 1996 Nail Studio (stated, confirmed by a reviewer), SuNam (reception). At LENA, Liti and Thao Nails, reviewers mention translation apps.

How much is a gel manicure in Da Nang?

200K to 250K VND (about $8 to $10): Reborn, 1996 and Liti 200K, Posh 240K with the manicure, SuNam 250K.
