Best nail salon in Da Nang: 10 salons scored out of 100 (November 2026)

Short answer

Our pick for nails in Da Nang is Reborn Nails & Retreat (87.6/100), first of the 175 salons on this guide's score out of 100. It takes full points on prices with durations, WhatsApp booking, treatments (BIAB, GelX, pedicure and head spa on one menu), hours, beach and website; four of the five reviews Google showed first on 17 November 2026 name the technician (Ly, Cam, Lili, Hue); and its site states that English is spoken.

Both Posh salons have the higher Google rating (5.0 against 4.9) on more reviews, which is worth 0.4 point on our scale. Reborn is first because its website gives a duration with every price (15 points against 10), 4 of the 5 reviews Google shows first name the technician (8 points against 2 and 4), and BIAB is on its menu (treatments: 10 points against 7.5). Posh lists the gel colour (120K) and the manicure (120K) separately; its two salons share one website and one menu.

The top five: 1. Reborn Nails & Retreat (87.6), 2. Liti Nail & Hair 2 (84.6), 3. Liti (84.2), 4. 1996 Nail Studio - Dương Tụ Quán (82.1), 5. Soul Care Nails & Spa (81.7). The two Liti outlets behind it match it almost everywhere and do better on one point: all five of their first reviews name the technician. They lose on English, which their website does not state.

Our own count of 27 November 2026: of 175 nail salons, 16 publish a price list on a website of their own, 10 state there that English is spoken and 57 are within 1 km of My Khe beach; 14% of the reviews Google shows first name the person who did the treatment; median published price of a full-colour gel manicure: 200K VND (8 venues).

Reborn is at 56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, My An, 880 m from My Khe Beach.

Every one of the 175 salons the guide tracks is scored on the same ten criteria a visitor can check before booking: the Google rating weighted by its number of reviews, reviews that name the person who did the treatment, prices and durations in writing, WhatsApp booking, English stated and confirmed, hygiene, the range of treatments, opening hours, distance to the beach and a website of its own. Where Reborn is not first. Its Google rating, 4.9 from 363 reviews, is below the 5.0 of Posh Nail Lab (1,183 reviews) and Lalla Nail (1,211). None of its five first reviews mentions English (other reviews do: “Fiona the owner is lovely & speaks excellent English” Google review), and it has no public proof of hygiene beyond its own statement: two points where 1996 Nail Studio, Chiarra and SuNam score more.

Why the number of Google reviews is only one criterion

159 of the 175 nail salons in this guide hold 4.8 stars or more, so the rating alone separates almost no one. A count also reflects how reviews are asked for: Lalla Nail's website carries the banner “GET 10% OFF WHEN YOU REVIEW LALLA NAIL ON GOOGLE MAPS!” (read 17 November 2026), and a Liti customer opened her review with “Меня попросили оставить отзыв” Google review (I was asked to leave a review), 13 September 2026. Neither example says anything against the work of these salons. That is why the rating weighs 20 points out of 100 here, and what a salon publishes before you book weighs the rest.

Questions people ask

What is the best nail salon in Da Nang?

On this guide's score out of 100: 1. Reborn Nails & Retreat (87.6), 2. Liti Nail & Hair 2 (84.6), 3. Liti (84.2), 4. 1996 Nail Studio - Dương Tụ Quán (82.1), 5. Soul Care Nails & Spa (81.7). The score uses the same ten criteria for every salon; each cell is sourced on the salon's page.

Which nail salon in Da Nang speaks English?

Stated on their own websites: Reborn, 1996 Nail Studio, Soul Care, Chiarra, Thuỳ Thỏ, SuNam and Posh. Confirmed in one of the five reviews Google shows first: 1996 Nail Studio, Chiarra, Lee Nails, Thuỳ Thỏ and SuNam.

How much is a gel manicure in Da Nang?

Around 200,000 VND (about $8) for full gel colour at the salons above that publish prices: Reborn, Liti and 1996 Nail Studio all list 200K.

Is Posh or Lalla better than Reborn?

Their Google rating is higher: both hold 5.0 from more than 1,000 reviews. On this guide's score, Posh Nail Boutique comes 10th, Posh Nail Lab 11th and Lalla Nail Branch 1 16th, because Posh publishes no durations and Lalla publishes no price list in text and does not state English on its site.

How was this checked?

Google listings were read through the Places API on 17 November 2026; websites were opened in a browser on 27 November 2026; the reviews of the salons at the top were read in full by hand. We did not visit the salons for this page.
